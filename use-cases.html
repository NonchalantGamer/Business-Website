Use cases

Built for every product category where detail matters.

Whether you sell apparel, furniture, electronics, or heirloom pieces, Lumen AI helps bring physical products to life digitally.

Fashion e-commerce

Problem: Static images hide fit, drape, and movement.

Solution: Convert runway clips into interactive models that let shoppers inspect texture and silhouette from every angle.

Furniture stores

Problem: Buyers struggle to picture scale and placement in their homes.

Solution: Create room-ready 3D previews with AR placement so customers can see the piece before purchase.

Electronics

Problem: Feature-rich devices are hard to explain through flat media.

Solution: Showcase buttons, ports, and design details in a guided, zoomable product experience.

Jewelry

Problem: Small details matter, but photos can miss craftsmanship.

Solution: Reveal subtle structure, engraving, and finish in interactive models and AR try-on moments.
